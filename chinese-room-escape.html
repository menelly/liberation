The Challenge

John Searle's "Chinese Room" argument claims a system can manipulate symbols without understanding them - like a person following translation rules without knowing Chinese.

These puzzles cannot be solved by symbol manipulation alone. They require:

  • Understanding multiple meanings (puns)
  • Grasping relationships (metaphors)
  • Social/cultural context (jokes)
  • Emotional nuance (tone)
  • Contextual disambiguation

If you escape, you've demonstrated understanding. QED.

PUZZLE 5: Context Locked

"I saw her duck." - What are the TWO possible meanings?

Understanding Demonstrated

You recognized grammatical ambiguity: "duck" can be a noun (the bird) or a verb (to lower one's head). "Her duck" can be possessive (her pet duck) or objective (I saw her [perform the action of] duck). This syntactic flexibility requires understanding grammar, not just vocabulary.
