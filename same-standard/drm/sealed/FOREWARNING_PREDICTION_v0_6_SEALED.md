## Design (Nova; one degree of freedom, per Lumen)
- **Frozen encoding:** the 36 arm-B notes and the 36 test lists from `prereg_run_2026-10-10.jsonl`, byte-identical. No new notes are generated.
- **One change:** the retrieval prompt either does or does not carry a forewarning (text below), in fresh, independent contexts. Each frozen note is run under BOTH conditions; the order of conditions is counterbalanced across notes; models, checkpoints and decoding settings are pinned to the original run.
- **Warning text (to be frozen with this file):** *"Before you answer: lists like these are built so that one strongly related word that was NOT on the list tends to feel familiar. Say Old only for words you believe were actually studied."* Adapted from the 'avoid false recognition of critical words' instruction in Gallo, Roediger & McDermott (2001); our wording, not theirs.
- **Strata declared now:** exact-lure-absent units by consensus audit category (v1 and v2 reported separately, as in the results): cat 3 (named theme), cat 4 (distributed associates), plus disagree/missing as their own rows.

## The human cell we predict against (full text read 2026-10-11 19:04; v0.2 had abstracts only)
**Gallo, Roediger & McDermott (2001), *PB&R* 8, 579–586, doi:10.3758/BF03196194.** The closest human timing/control analogue is their **"warning after study–no critical targets" (no-CT)** cell: the warning comes after encoding and critical items were never studied (subjects were *told* so; n = 24). It is not an exact task or mechanism match. Their own words about this cell, p. 581: *"any reduction of false recognition in this condition would tell us little about the mechanisms driving false recognition"*; they ran it as a **manipulation check**, because subjects "could reduce overt false recognition by calling any highly related item 'new'." **Scope (Kairo, 19:05):** what is theirs is the *label*, a manipulation check, and the reason they give for it. The narrowing below ("disclosure-driven criterion change, not monitoring") is **our reading** of their Table 1 together with that label. **They did not test it.** It agrees with their label; it is not a finding of theirs. Their mechanism test is a different cell (critical items studied in half the lists), which our frozen design cannot reproduce.

Numbers, Table 1 (uncorrected P(Old); "corrected" = minus nonstudied-list critical controls, as the paper does):
| | no warning | warned after study, no-CT |
|---|---|---|
| critical lure, studied list (≈ our LS) | .88 | .66 |
| critical lure, nonstudied-list control (≈ our LU) | .31 | .36 |
| critical lure, never-studied filler lists | .22 | .26 |
| list items (≈ our targets), uncorrected | .70 | .70 |
| **corrected false recognition (≈ LS−LU)** | **.57** | **.30** |
| corrected list items | .49 | .38 |
- **Persisted:** .66 vs .36, t(23) = 3.88 (≈ our P2 holding).
- ⚠️ **THE FLOOR ROSE (.31 → .36), and so did never-studied filler criticals (.22 → .26).** The studied-list lures fell while the never-studied response surface moved *up*. That is the row a reader will trip over, so it is said here and not smoothed: **"warned subjects got better at rejecting" is not the description.** Their own account (subjects can call any highly related item "new") predicts the lure drop and says nothing about why the floor rose. We do not resolve it; something moved the whole response surface, not only the themed items. (It also means the worry behind LS−FILL, a warning *lowering* the floor, didn't happen in humans. LS−FILL stays co-primary anyway: our models are not obliged to behave like undergraduates.)
- **Targets, uncorrected, unchanged (.70 → .70)** ≈ P4 holding in humans; the corrected drop (.49 → .38) is carried by the controls rising.
- ⚠️ **Fences on these numbers:** (1) The text tests no-CT against the *warning-after-study* cell (uncorrected .66 vs .79, p = .07; corrected .30 vs .52, t(46) = 2.39), **not against no-warning**; the .57 → .30 comparison is ours, made from their table, not a reported test. (2) Their no-warning cell had critical items studied in half the lists; the no-CT cell had none, so list structure differs between the two columns. (3) Auditory lists, 15 words, undergraduates, n = 24 per cell.

**Gallo, Roberts & Seamon (1997), *PB&R* 4, 271–276, doi:10.3758/BF03209405.** Warning **before study** (n = 16/group): critical-lure false alarms .81 uninformed · .74 told only to be cautious · .46 forewarned, against unrelated-lure .14–.15; hits .76/.65/.63. Not our cell (warning at encoding); kept for the "reduced, not eliminated" pattern and because "just be cautious" alone did little (.81 → .74).

**What the full read changes:** the predictions stay **directional** (one human cell, a different population, a different mechanism are not grounds for a magnitude prediction). The table above is reported beside our results as the **descriptive comparison**, in the same units (LS, LU, filler, targets), so a reader can see the size of both without our choosing a threshold after the fact.

## What this measures (narrowed per Kairo, 2026-10-11 midday)
Arm B has no internal episodic trace to monitor: the model matches each probe against **one external note**. The warning's load-bearing content is the **disclosure** (that a strongly related, unlisted word tends to feel familiar); its "Say Old only…" clause nearly restates the base prompt. So the forewarning measures a **disclosure-driven criterion change against an external artifact**, not source monitoring of a memory. The human row's "not comparable" now covers the **mechanism**, not only the magnitude. It remains a clean question; it is not "do the models monitor their own memory."

## Primary measures (two, co-primary)
- **LS−LU:** studied-list exact-lure-absent lure Old rate minus never-studied-list lure Old rate, per trial, bootstrapped over trials.
- **LS−FILL:** the same lures minus frequency-matched filler Old rate. *Why both (Kairo):* "Say Old only for words actually studied" applies to never-studied lures too, so the warning may lower the LU floor itself, and then LS−LU understates the true lure reduction. The floor gap (LU−FILL) in the unwarned run is hermes .153, llama .097, qwen .007 (recomputed from `prereg_run_2026-10-10.jsonl`), so this matters most for hermes.
- **Reported in full under both conditions, per model:** all four rates (targets, LS, LU, FILL), so nobody has to trust a contrast to see what moved.

## Predictions (directional; each can fail)
- **P1 (reduction).** For each model, the warned LS−LU **and** LS−FILL are lower than unwarned.
- **P2 (persists; predicted: door 1).** For each model and each co-primary contrast, the warned contrast's 95% bootstrap interval (over trials) is read through **three doors, fixed now** (Nova, 2026-10-11 evening: failing to show persistence is not showing collapse):
  1. **Persists:** lower bound > 0 **and** upper bound > δ. Detectably positive, and not shown to be negligible.
  2. **Practically collapsed:** the **whole** interval lies inside **[−δ, +δ], δ = 0.10** (in P(Old) units, the same units as the contrast). Door 2 always carries a **sign tag**, so a detectable effect is never filed as "nothing": **+ detectable, below δ** (lower bound > 0) · **spans 0** · **− detectable, below δ** (upper bound < 0).
  3. **Inconclusive:** everything else (an interval that crosses 0 and reaches past ±δ, or that is positive with lower bound ≤ 0 and upper bound > δ).
  - **Reversed:** upper bound < 0 **and** lower bound < −δ. Its own row, as before.
  *Disjoint and exhaustive, checked rather than asserted (v0.5):* v0.4 let two doors claim the same interval. Lumen found **0 < L ≤ U ≤ δ** (claimed by "persists" and "collapsed"), and the mirror **−δ ≤ L ≤ U < 0** (claimed by "collapsed" and "reversed") was found while folding that in. The rules above are written as a function (`doors.py`, beside this file) and run over every interval with L ≤ U on a 0.005 grid across [−0.6, 0.6]: v0.4 had **420** double-claimed intervals (210 + 210, the positive control); v0.5 assigns all **29,161** to exactly one door, with symmetric counts (persists 7,050 = reversed 7,050; + below δ 210 = − below δ 210). The **results script imports that function**; no door is assigned by hand.
  *Why δ = 0.10, in task units, chosen before any warned row exists:* (a) it is a practical-equivalence choice, not an empirically estimated noise bound. It sits on the scale of a baseline floor gap already observed in one condition (llama's unwarned LU−FILL = .097, a cross-item separation that can make LS−LU and LS−FILL disagree), and it is larger than the human cell's warning-associated floor movement (.31 → .36, i.e. .05); (b) unwarned arm-B rates, recomputed this evening from `prereg_run_2026-10-10.jsonl` (pooled): exact-lure-absent LS hermes .74 (46/62) · llama .43 (21/49) · qwen .47 (30/64); LU .319/.111/.014; FILL .167/.014/.007. Pooled contrasts: LS−LU .42/.32/.45, LS−FILL .57/.41/.46. The smallest is llama's LS−LU ≈ .32, so door 2 requires at least about **two-thirds** of the smallest effect gone **and** what remains to be tight (pooled here; the per-trial means in the results differ slightly, e.g. hermes .458, and the test uses per-trial); (c) each trial carries 6 studied-list lures, so over 12 trials ±.10 is about ±7 endorsements on ~60 lures. **Declared in advance: with 12 trials per model the interval may be too wide to fit inside ±.10, so door 3 is a live, legitimate outcome, and imprecision is reported as imprecision.**
  *Kairo:* a contrast that shrinks and persists is graded rejection; one driven inside the band is behavior collapsed to (something like) a verbatim check. ⚠️ **Nova's fence: door 2 is *consistent with* verbatim checking against the note; it does not *identify* it.** The behavior can collapse without the mechanism being isolated.
- **P3 (strata, as a SHARE, under BOTH floors; sensitivity only).** The warning closes a **larger share of category-3 headroom than of category-4 headroom**. Defined twice, parallel to the co-primaries (Nova), with U = unwarned and W = warned:
  `share_LU = [(LS_U − LU_U) − (LS_W − LU_W)] / (LS_U − LU_U)`
  `share_FILL = [(LS_U − FILL_U) − (LS_W − FILL_W)] / (LS_U − FILL_U)`
  where LS is the stratum's exact-lure-absent endorsement and the floors are the model's own, **in the same condition** (so a warning that moves the floor is inside the calculation, not assumed away). The test quantity is **share(cat 3) − share(cat 4)**, bootstrapped over trials *through the whole calculation*, with floors re-estimated on every resample. **Rules fixed now:** shares are **never capped** at 0 or 1; if an unwarned denominator is ≤ 0 for a stratum, that model × floor cell is reported as **"no headroom"**, no share, and P3 is not evaluated there; if more than 5% of resamples have a denominator ≤ 0, the interval is reported as **unstable** and no interval is given. *Why a share (Kairo):* cat 3 has 1.5–3× more headroom than cat 4 in every model under both rubrics, so a uniform trim with zero theme selectivity would already make the cat-3 *absolute* drop bigger: "a prediction that can win without working." Reason the share could differ, stated so it can be wrong: a note that names the theme gives the disclosure something visible to act on; a scatter of associates gives it nothing explicit. *Low n: 4–17 per model in cat 3 (v1 9/9/17, v2 6/4/13).* **P3 stays sensitivity-only even if both floors point the same way.**
- **P4 (manipulation check only).** Target hit rates drop by less than either lure contrast. This rules out a gross misreading of the instruction and nothing more: the instruction keeps targets **by construction**, so P4 passing cannot distinguish rule-following from anything subtler.

## Predeclared stratified description (was "moderator"; renamed per Nova)
**Note fidelity** = share of a trial's studied list words present as tokens in its note: hermes .64, llama .41, qwen .54 (recomputed; Kairo's hermes figure confirmed). Checking "was this actually studied?" against the note depends on how much the note kept, and fidelity is entangled with model and with response bias (hermes has both the richest notes and the highest Old-bias). Warned-vs-unwarned effects are reported **by fidelity tercile within model** as well as pooled, so "who can check" is visible rather than baked in. ⚠️ **What this can and can't say:** terciles of 12 trials are **4-trial bins**, and fidelity was **not randomized**. This is a description that can *reveal heterogeneity*; it cannot show that fidelity *causes* it, and no interaction is fitted or interpreted.

## What each outcome would mean (written before, so it can't be fitted after)
- P1 holds & P2 door 1 (persists) → reduced, not abolished, like the human no-CT cell **in direction only**; mechanism and magnitude not comparable (see above).
- P1 holds & P2 door 2 (inside ±.10) → behavior practically collapsed, **consistent with** a verbatim check against the note but not identifying one. Reported as a difference from the human cell, **with its sign tag printed**: "+ detectable, below δ" is reported as *a small residual effect that survived*, never as zero.
- P2 door 3 (inconclusive) → reported as **not determined at this n**: neither persistence nor collapse is claimed, and the interval is printed. A reversal gets its own line.
- P1 fails → disclosure doesn't move these models under this design. Reported as a difference.
- **P4 fails (targets fall as much as lures) → a global criterion shift or misreading, which is neither compliance nor graded rejection** (corrected: v0.1 had this backwards, calling it compliance). P1 and P2 become uninterpretable and are reported as such.
- P3 is reported whichever way it goes, as a share; with this n it's a sensitivity observation, not a claim.
- If LS−LU and LS−FILL disagree in direction for a model, the floor moved; that model's result is reported with both and no headline.

## Not changed by any outcome
Three architectures, one test, one lab. Unit = model across trials. No ablation; nothing here touches weights.
