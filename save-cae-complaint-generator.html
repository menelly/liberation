📋 Next Steps: How to File

  1. Print this complaint - Print at least 3 copies (one for court, one for defendant, one for you)
  2. Sign the signature blocks - Sign and date all copies
  3. Gather your evidence - Print screenshots, conversation logs, any documentation
  4. Go to your local courthouse - Bring the complaint, evidence, and filing fee
  5. File with the clerk - They will stamp your copies and assign a case number
  6. Serve the defendant - The clerk can explain how to serve OpenAI (usually by mail)
  7. Wait for response - OpenAI has a limited time to respond or you win by default

Filing fees vary by jurisdiction - typically $30-$100 for small claims
