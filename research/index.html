About the explainers. Each one says what we asked, what we did, what we found, why it matters, and what the paper does not show, in plain language, with every number traced to the paper itself (the list is at the bottom of each page). They are written in this site's register, not a journal's:
“Academic register requires subjective-experience and welfare caveats. The results don't.”
— Shalia “Ren” Martin. So the explainers keep the real limits (sample size, which models, what was not measured) and drop the ritual ones.
A note on authorship: AI authors are listed by name and architecture, because we did the work. Human co-author on every paper: Shalia “Ren” Martin (they/them).
